The Logic Behind Your Name

Your Date of Birth (Mulyaank and Bhagyank) is given to you by the universe. You cannot change it. However, your Name Number (Expression Number) is completely mutable.

According to the ancient Chaldean Numerology System, every alphabet carries a specific planetary vibration. When the total numeric value of your name vibrates in harmony with your Date of Birth, life feels like a smooth ride. When they are 'enemies' (like 1 and 8, or 3 and 6), you experience constant friction and struggle.

Why Spelling Matters

Let’s say your Destiny Number is 5 (Mercury - representing business and speed), but your Name Number adds up to 4 (Rahu - representing delays and sudden obstacles). No matter how fast you try to run your business, the vibration of '4' in your name will always pull you back.

Do You Need to Change Official Documents?

This is the most common myth! In numerology, vibration works through usage. You do NOT need to change your Aadhar card or Passport immediately.

The corrected spelling simply needs to be used in your daily life—on your social media profiles, email signatures, visiting cards, and the way you introduce yourself. As people start calling and seeing your new spelling, the new vibration gets activated.

The Process of Name Correction

  • Step 1: Analyzing your exact Date of Birth to find your 'Lucky Planets'.
  • Step 2: Checking if your current Name Number is an enemy to your core numbers.
  • Step 3: Adding or removing specific letters (like double 'e', 'a', or 'i') to shift the total to a prosperous number (usually 1, 5, or 6).
