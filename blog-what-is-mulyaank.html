What is Mulyaank (Psychic Number)? How to Calculate & Its Impact

Dr. Jyoti Srivastava • Published: March 2026

Have you ever wondered why you instantly connect with some people, while others drain your energy? Or why certain dates always seem to bring you good news?

In Vedic Numerology, the secret to your behavior, choices, and initial life experiences lies in one single digit: your Mulyaank.

What exactly is Mulyaank?

Also known as the Psychic Number or Driver Number, your Mulyaank is the number that governs your mind. While your Destiny Number (Bhagyank) dictates your ultimate life goal, your Mulyaank represents who you are on a daily basis.

It controls your thoughts, your food habits, your choice of friends, and your immediate reactions to stress. Because it is connected to a specific planet, your Mulyaank heavily influences your personality traits.

How to Calculate Your Mulyaank

Calculating your Psychic Number is extremely simple. You only need the Day of your birth (not the month or year).

  • If you were born between the 1st and 9th of any month, that number itself is your Mulyaank.
  • If you were born on a double-digit date (10th to 31st), simply add the two digits together.

Example: If you were born on the 24th of August, your calculation is: 2 + 4 = 6. Your Mulyaank is 6.

Quick Overview of Mulyaank 1 to 9

Every number from 1 to 9 is ruled by a specific planet (Navagraha). Here is a quick snapshot of what your number says about you:

  • Number 1 (Ruled by Sun): You are a natural leader. Independent, ambitious, and slightly egoistic. You hate working under others.
  • Number 2 (Ruled by Moon): You are deeply emotional and intuitive. A peacemaker who values relationships over money, but prone to mood swings.
  • Number 3 (Ruled by Jupiter): Highly creative and knowledgeable. You are an excellent communicator and advisor.
  • Number 4 (Ruled by Rahu): Practical, hardworking, and rebellious. You often face sudden struggles but have immense fighting spirit.
  • Number 5 (Ruled by Mercury): The ultimate business mind. You love travel, freedom, and fast-paced environments.
  • Number 6 (Ruled by Venus): Drawn to luxury, beauty, and family. You are naturally magnetic and love taking care of people.
  • Number 7 (Ruled by Ketu): The deep thinker. You are spiritual, analytical, and love spending time alone researching.
  • Number 8 (Ruled by Saturn): You are the powerhouse of wealth and administration. Your life involves delays, but massive success comes through hard work.
  • Number 9 (Ruled by Mars): Aggressive, energetic, and humanitarian. You have a strong sense of justice and a short temper.

Why Should You Care About Your Mulyaank?

Understanding your Mulyaank is the first step in Vedic Numerology. It helps you:

  1. Find Compatible Partners: Some numbers naturally clash (like 1 and 8). Knowing this helps avoid toxic business and romantic relationships.
  2. Pick Lucky Dates: Planning a product launch or a job interview? Doing it on a date friendly to your Mulyaank increases success rates.
  3. Select the Right Colors: Wearing your ruling planet's colors enhances your aura and confidence.

Want to know your exact Life Path and Destiny?

Your Mulyaank is just 30% of the story. Your Bhagyank (Destiny) and Name Number dictate the remaining 70%. If they are not aligned, life feels like a constant struggle.
