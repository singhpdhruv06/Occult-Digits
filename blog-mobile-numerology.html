Mobile Numerology: Is Your Phone Number Attracting Wealth?

Dr. Jyoti Srivastava • Published: March 2026

In today's digital era, your mobile number is your primary point of contact. Every time a client calls you, a business deal is negotiated, or an OTP is received, the vibration of those 10 digits is activated.

Mobile Numerology is the science of analyzing these 10 digits to see if they are bringing you prosperity, or silently causing communication breakdowns and financial losses.

How to Check Your Mobile Total

Simply add all the 10 digits of your phone number together until you get a single-digit number (1 to 9).
Example: If your number is 9876543210: 9+8+7+6+5+4+3+2+1+0 = 45 = 4+5 = 9.

The Best Numbers for Wealth

  • Total 5 (Mercury): This is the ultimate number for business owners, traders, and salespeople. It brings fast networking, dynamic communication, and quick money flow.
  • Total 6 (Venus): Best for people in media, fashion, luxury, or those seeking harmonious family relationships. Number 6 attracts premium, high-paying clients.
  • Total 1 (Sun): Ideal for politicians, CEOs, and senior managers. It gives authority and leadership.

Numbers to Avoid in Business

Generally, phone totals of 4 (Rahu) and 8 (Saturn) should be avoided. Number 4 causes sudden obstacles and misunderstandings in communication. Number 8 brings extreme delays—your payments will likely get stuck, and clients will negotiate endlessly.

It's Not Just About the Total!

While the total matters, the pairings of numbers within the mobile number are equally crucial. Having sequences like '48' or '28' inside the number can cause stress and legal issues, even if the grand total is a lucky 5!
