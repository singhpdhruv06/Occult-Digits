Missing Numbers in Lo-Shu Grid: How to Find Your Energy Blocks

Dr. Jyoti Srivastava • Published: March 2026

Write down your exact Date of Birth (e.g., 14-07-1990). Notice how certain digits from 1 to 9 do not appear in your birth date? In the magical science of the Lo-Shu Grid (a 3x3 numerological square), these absent digits are called "Missing Numbers."

Missing numbers represent specific elemental energies (Water, Earth, Wood, Metal, Fire) that you lack at birth. This gap creates specific behavioral patterns and life obstacles.

Impact of Common Missing Numbers

Missing Number 5 (Earth Element)

Number 5 sits exactly in the center of the Lo-Shu grid. It represents balance, core stability, and business acumen. People missing a 5 often struggle with self-confidence, have scattered focus, and face sudden ups and downs in their career.

Missing Number 6 (Metal Element)

Number 6 is the number of Venus (luxury, family, and support from friends/superiors). If 6 is missing, you might find that no matter how much you earn, saving money is hard. It also causes delays in marriage or a lack of support from your father/boss.

Can We Fix Missing Numbers?

Yes! While you cannot change your date of birth, you can definitely introduce the missing element into your life through powerful numerological and Vastu remedies:

  • Name Correction: Spelling your name so that its total matches the missing lucky number.
  • Crystal & Color Therapy: Wearing a specific crystal bracelet (like Pyrite or Rose Quartz) to compensate for missing elements.
  • Mobile & Asset Numerology: Choosing a phone or vehicle number that carries the vibration of your missing number.
